Safety Limits

  • OSC volumes: -50 to +40 dB (prevents clipping)
  • Delay feedback: max +25 (prevents runaway feedback)
  • All FX: max +80 (safe levels)
  • Long envelopes: max 80 (prevents extremely long tails)
  • Standard OSCs only by default (DX7/sample require manual selection)
  • Sample/Wavetable: auto-selected from chosen folder + subfolders
Tip: Limits are configurable in js/patchmorph.js (MORPH_LIMITS object)
